As a child I had a little Casio keyboard, no idea if I had wished for it, or if it was even a gift to me or to my brother. Last summer my parents asked, if they could dispose of it. It was falling apart: You had to apply pressure where it plugged in, so it would get any power. Also some keys were falling out. 

I had to reply: "Let's throw it away". But I didn't do so, before going through every sound and every rhythm and recording it on my iPhone with the iPhone microphone. I then used these sounds to create sample instruments in Logic.

The resultof this work, is this little sketch. I don't think it's particularly good or interesting, but the sounds instill nostalgia in me. Part of me regrets throwing the Casio away, as I have seen people using them to circuit bend, but then again, you have to let of things to make space for other things.
