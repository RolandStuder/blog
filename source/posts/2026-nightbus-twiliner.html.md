I travel 4 - 6 times from Spain to Switzerland each year, my options:

- Flying: Easyjet has direct flight connections between Sevilla and Geneva most days of the week, most of the time pretty cheap. A good option,not very environmental.
- Train: Not feasible, too many changes, too much risk, no nighttrain options.
- Night bus (Flixbus): There are Flixbus night buses between Geneva and Barcelona, then Barcelona, combining it with a high speed train is tolerable, the buses seats are only minimally inclinable, so it makes for a tough night.

Now there is a new option:

## Twiliner Night Buses:  Bus with bussiness class style seats

For half a year Twiliner runs nightly buses from Switzerland to Barcelona and Amsterdam. The buses have a total of 24 seats, 8 rows of three seats each. Each seat converts into a bed, you can lie and sleep fully horizontally. Prices are 170€ - 210€ for a one way trip. I tried and it was quite nice:

- You get a blanket and everything, there is a spacious changing room to slip into your pyjamas.
- Bathroom is quite nice, not your typical 



Two times I took a nightbus from Geneva to Barcelona and then the train. Not very comfortable, but tolerable, if you can book the seat next to you for a little fee, so you got two seats for yourself..
